## Baseline note (M0, locked rule)
The locked rule 'any determinant of the drug class -> R' collapses to BA ~0.50 for E. coli ceftazidime and K. pneumoniae ciprofloxacin because intrinsic chromosomal genes (e.g. blaEC, oqxAB) count as determinants. Kept as-is and reported: naive class-level rules are not a usable baseline for these cells.
