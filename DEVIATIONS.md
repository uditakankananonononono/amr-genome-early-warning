# Deviations and clarifications (logged after gate lock)
1. G4 "first seen after cutoff" is computed as the first collection year the allele appears in the two NCBI Pathogen Detection PDG snapshots used here (results/bla_allele_first_year.tsv), not the year the allele was first described in the literature. Some alleles (e.g. blaIMP-18, blaSHV-57) were published earlier; they are "new to this surveillance stream", which is what an early-warning system sees.
2. G4 gate test had n=5 novel alleles in date-test isolates (1 in a false negative), below the n>=10 threshold -> reported descriptively as locked. An additional post-2019 vs pre-2020 allele comparison (all alleles observed in PDG, 4 families) was run and is labeled EXPLORATORY in results; it did not influence any locked gate.
3. Structures: KPC-2 2OV5, SHV-1 1SHV, NDM-1 4EYL, IMP-1 1DD6 (RCSB). Site = Ser70 OG (class A, verified residue 70 is SER in both files) or all Zn ions (MBL).
